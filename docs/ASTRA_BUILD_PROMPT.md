# Songa — Astra creative and production prompt

Prepared from the creator's eight uploaded reference files at commit dbf45021eaf62501daca448421e47c26eb76e071.

The newly supplied Vision, Overview and Gameplay Identity specify stylized **3D, third-person running and three lanes**. This prompt follows that direction. Earlier side-on 2D prototype decisions and developer-written design notes are historical, not the current design authority.

## Copyable prompt

Create **Songa: Rise of the Silverback**, a distinctive, character-led, stylized **3D endless runner for iOS and Android**, presented in portrait orientation with a third-person camera following behind Songa. Build around readable three-lane running and responsive one-thumb controls.

Treat these creator-supplied files as the design source of truth:

- Songa_Rise_of_the_Silverback_Game_Vision.docx — premise, story, tone and gameplay ambitions.
- Songa Game Overview.png — world, character appeal and overall feature identity.
- Songa Core Gameplay Identity 1.png — rear gameplay camera, three lanes, left/right swipe switching, fruits and power items.
- Songa Art Direction.png — shapes, proportions, materials, palette, motifs and environment direction.
- songa-cut.png and logo.png — approved hero and branding references.
- startup-portrait.png — intended menu composition and visual richness.
- startup-landscape 1.png — the mountain, volcanic, waterfall and forest scenery reference. Despite its name, this image is portrait.

### Creative promise

Make the player feel like a young gorilla prince escaping through a living, mysterious mountain kingdom: agile, physically powerful, vulnerable and determined. Combine fast, satisfying movement with wonder, emotional stakes and cinematic momentum. The tone is adventurous, energetic and mysterious, with broad family appeal.

The world is a **fictional gorilla civilization inspired by Volcanoes National Park, Kinigi, Musanze, Rwanda**, and the misty mountain gorilla regions of East Africa. Establish Rwanda through the landscape, materials, approved patterns, wildlife and musical direction throughout the experience.

### Songa and the visual standard

Match Songa's supplied design: warm copper/chestnut-brown fur, tall swept-up crest, large expressive brown eyes, strong eyebrows, tan face and chest, oversized hands and feet, a powerful youthful upper body, and a cobalt-blue tied waist cloth. Preserve his cheeky confidence, warmth and determined expression. Keep the same identity and proportions in front, side and rear views.

Use rounded, appealing forms and expressive animation with the warmth and readability described in the Art Direction board. The Temple Run Legends and Jumanji references establish adventure, atmosphere and finish; Songa must have his own recognizable world and personality.

The art needs depth, rich lighting, convincing materials and clear silhouettes on a small phone. Translate the reference fur into a mobile-efficient stylized treatment. Give running, turns, jumps, slides, landings and strength actions distinct poses, weight, anticipation and follow-through. Make Songa's hands, shoulders, face, crest and cloth contribute to the motion.

### Story and pursuit

Begin **Level 1: The Exile** in chaos within the **Emerald Canopy Kingdom**. Songa is a young silverback prince-in-training forced to flee after the rogue silverback **Karumo** seizes control. Move directly into play; establish the situation through action and environmental storytelling.

During Level 1, Karumo is mostly unseen. Build his presence through thunderous footsteps, roars, breaking trees, collapsing terrain and occasional enormous shadows or glimpses through fog. His threat should create urgency and mystery. Reveal the hidden history of Songa's father and Karumo's mystery gradually.

Use the startup artwork to guide the title scene's composition. Clearly distinguish the richly composed menu scene from the moving gameplay camera.

### Gameplay identity

The immediate loop is **collect, dodge, survive**: automatic forward running; three readable lanes; swipe left/right to change lanes; fruit and power-item routes that encourage decisions; increasing challenge with fair reaction time and traversable obstacle sequences.

Jumping, sliding and dodging are part of the core. Up-swipe to jump and down-swipe to slide are proposed initial mappings; only left/right swipe switching is explicitly fixed by the supplied board.

Build the broader experience around the documented gorilla-specific abilities:

- Strength smashing and charging through destructible obstacles.
- Vine swinging and wall-climbing sequences.
- Animal companion assists and Inyambo cow power-ups.
- Rhythm/percussion-inspired speed boosts.
- Dynamic cinematic chase moments and vertical terrain.

Stage these features coherently. Keep ability triggers, companion effects, Inyambo effects, rhythm interaction, failure rules and scoring as explicitly documented design proposals until defined; do not inherit the prototype's three-heart or banana-only rules as established requirements.

### World and presentation

Create dense bamboo escape paths, ancient gorilla temple ruins, giant roots, sacred statues, misty forests, slippery volcanic cliffs, waterfall crossings, rope bridges and rain-soaked mountain trails. Expand into the fantasy volcanic landscapes shown in the reference direction.

Use lush forest greens, warm sunlight, earthen browns, mossy stone, cool mist and Songa's contrasting blue cloth. Compose depth through foreground foliage, midground architecture and distant mountains. Preserve a clear view of the running path and approaching hazards through lighting, silhouette and material contrast. Weather and cinematic effects must support readability.

Integrate creator-approved Rwandan patterns and motifs into appropriate cloth, carvings, architecture and interface details. Use original or licensed music informed by approved Rwandan musical references, supported by birds, jungle ambience, waterfalls, footfalls and the sound of Karumo's pursuit.

Retain the supplied gold/wood/silver logo. Derive the menu's carved wood, dark stone, leaves and generous primary Play button from startup-portrait.png. Build working interface elements independently of the flattened reference picture. Keep the in-run interface compact.

### First delivery and honest production scope

Start with a focused, polished, playable section of **The Exile**: a bamboo-to-ruins escape with faithful animated Songa, a rear three-lane camera, lane changes, jumping, sliding, fruits/power items, one gorilla-strength destruction interaction, one restrained Karumo sound/shadow moment, and functioning failure/restart.

This is a proposed first production milestone. Vine traversal, wall climbing, detailed companion and Inyambo systems, percussion mechanics, additional regions and weather remain documented expansion goals.

Create an explicit asset plan covering the Songa model, rig and animation clips, environment kit, materials, effects, audio and interface elements. The uploaded PNGs are visual and branding references; they are not already-built animated 3D characters or interactive scenes. Keep completed work, temporary assets and planned work clearly identified.

Provide an easy-to-open web preview for creator feedback and keep that separate from signed Android/iOS builds. Define target devices before claiming mobile performance; verify touch controls, frame pacing, restart and obstacle fairness on representative hardware.

### Acceptance standard

A paused gameplay frame must visibly belong to the supplied Art Direction and Gameplay Identity boards: recognizable Songa, a richly lit world and a clear three-lane trail receding ahead. A short gameplay sequence must demonstrate responsive control, expressive movement, physical gorilla strength, and Karumo's mysterious pursuit.

Evaluate the first slice for **visual fidelity, character identity, game feel and readable adventure**. A collection of named features or passing mechanical checks is not evidence that this creative target has been met.
