# First playable: design decisions

## Agreed direction

- Name: **Songa — Rise of the Silverback**.
- Genre: 2D endless runner for iOS and Android.
- Orientation: **portrait**, designed for one-handed play.
- Setting: **Volcanoes National Park**, Kinigi, Musanze, Rwanda; Virunga-inspired peaks, bamboo forests, volcanic rock, and mountain mist.
- Tone: playful and family-friendly. The pursuer catches up without combat, gore, or a frightening defeat animation.
- Protagonist: Songa, a young gorilla; pursuer: a larger silverback.
- Language: English first. Kinyarwanda is optional and should be reviewed before inclusion.

## Core loop

Start a run → jump over rocks/logs → slide under low bamboo boughs → collect bananas → survive increasing speed → review distance and try again.

The hero stays at a fixed horizontal position while the world scrolls. Each encounter can be solved with one action. Early runs introduce two ground hazards before a branch. Three hearts allow mistakes; grace after a hit prevents one obstacle taking multiple hearts. The adult silverback gets closer when Songa loses hearts. Bananas are arcade collectibles, not a statement about mountain gorilla feeding practices.

## Art direction

Warm morning light, sage and deep forest greens, soft gold highlights, layered peaks and mist. Keep scenery recognizably inspired by the named park rather than showing an erupting volcano. Strong silhouettes and a clear trail keep gameplay legible on a small phone.

The supplied gold/wood/silver logo informs the warm gold accents. The approved logo file and separate Songa character reference still need to be incorporated. The current procedural characters are placeholders so gameplay can be evaluated now.

## Culture and story, for the next art pass

The setting, place names, and Rwandan identity are established. Specific cultural patterns, instruments, clothing, stories, and Kinyarwanda lines should follow the creator's references and review. No unreviewed local-language translation or claim of cultural authenticity is included. The park setting does not imply endorsement by park authorities.

Useful next inputs: the Songa character image, transparent logo artwork, animation/style references, and a short explanation of why the adult is chasing Songa. These can refine the prototype without blocking core gameplay.

## Deferred production decisions

Final character animation and audio; detailed cultural content; accessibility and safe-area polish on target phones; difficulty tuned with players; monetization and audience age range; achievements and progression; translations; app identity and signing; store listings and device QA. No ad SDK, payment system, account system, or backend is added by default.
